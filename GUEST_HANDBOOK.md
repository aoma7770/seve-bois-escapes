## Waste Collection & Recycling

### Weekly Collection Schedule

**Monday Morning**
- Household waste (grey bin / conteneur gris)
- Organic waste (green bin / conteneur vert)
- *Action:* Place bins in front of property the evening before

**Tuesday Morning**
- Cardboard & paper (tied bundles or cardboard box)
- *Action:* Place out the evening before

**Glass (Special Instructions)**
Glass bottles, jars, and containers are **NOT collected at your door**. You must take glass to the glass bubble located in Laforêt:

> **Location:** In front of the "La Sapinière" building, going up towards the woods (en montant dans les bois)

There are two bubbles: one for white glass and one for coloured glass.

**PMC (Plastic, Metal, Cartons)**
- Check the commune calendar for collection dates
- PMC bags available at commune office (€2/roll)

### Recycling Centres (For Bulky Items)

**Parc à conteneurs de Vresse-sur-Semois**
- Address: Rue Grande, RN 945, Oizy (Bièvre)
- Phone: 061/46.60.22
- Hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 9:00–17:00
- Closed: Mondays and public holidays

**Parc à conteneurs de Malvoisin**
- Address: Route de Bouillon, Malvoisin
- Phone: 061/58.99.47
- Hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 9:00–17:00
- Closed: Mondays and public holidays

### Bulky Items

Bulky items are no longer collected door-to-door. Options:
1. Call **Ressourcerie Namuroise** at **081 260 400** — they collect from your home
2. Take items to the recycling centre (parc à conteneurs)
