## Design implications

The real photography confirms a strong **soft eco-luxe** direction rather than rustic chalet styling. The architecture is minimalist and contemporary, with exposed timber, large glazing, warm natural light, carefully chosen furniture, and restrained decorative accents. The palette visible in the photography aligns well with forest green, timber, off-white, slate, and terracotta accents.

The most compelling visual differentiators are the **eco-architectural timber shell**, the **large openings toward nature**, and the **refined interior styling**. These should shape the homepage hierarchy: immersive imagery first, then reassurance about comfort, then eco-credentials and booking trust signals.
